Newbound peers don't need one: they swap addresses over the encrypted
        Newbound peer network and connect directly (add one only if a very strict NAT still blocks them).
        Phones need one: they aren't on the peer network, so a network with phones needs at least one
        lighthouse with a public address and port the phone can reach from the internet.
        Changes apply when you save the configuration.
